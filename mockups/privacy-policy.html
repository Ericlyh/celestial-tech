01 · Who we are.

Celestial Tech Limited ("we", "us", "our") is a private company incorporated in Hong Kong (CR No. 3124-582). Our registered office is at 14/F, 18 Bonham Strand, Sheung Wan, Hong Kong. We are the data controller for any personal data collected through our services.

02 · What we collect.

It depends on which service you use. Generally, we collect only the minimum necessary:

  • Account data. Name, work email, phone number, business name, billing details.
  • Service data. Conversations between your customers and the deployed agent, system logs, performance metrics.
  • Website data. Server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for 30 days, then aggregated and discarded.

We do not collect biometric data, location data beyond what's required for service delivery, or any special-category data under PDPO.

We don't sell personal data. We don't share it with advertisers. We don't use it to train models that other customers will use.

03 · Why we collect it.

To provide the service you've contracted us for, to bill you, to comply with our legal obligations, and to detect abuse. We rely on the lawful bases of contract performance, legal obligation, and legitimate interest under PDPO.

04 · Where it lives.

All customer service data is stored on infrastructure physically located in Hong Kong, operated by HK-based providers. Backups are encrypted at rest and held within HK. We do not transfer personal data outside Hong Kong unless required by law (e.g. a valid HK court order).

05 · Who we share with.

  • Our staff. Only those with a need to know, and only with documented access controls.
  • Sub-processors. Listed below. We notify you of changes at least 30 days in advance.
  • Regulators. When required by HK law, nothing more.

06 · How long we keep it.

By default, conversation transcripts are retained for 90 days, then deleted. You may configure shorter or longer retention (up to 7 years) on the FIRM plan. Account data is retained for the duration of the contract plus 7 years for tax purposes.

07 · Your rights.

Under PDPO, you have the right to: access, correct, delete, and restrict processing of personal data we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days, free of charge.

To exercise any of these rights, write to privacy@celestial-tech.com. We will acknowledge within 5 business days.

08 · Cookies & tracking.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar. We use one self-hosted analytics tool (Plausible, EU instance) that records only aggregated, anonymised page views.

09 · Minors.

Our services are intended for business customers (B2B). We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10 · Changes to this policy.

We will notify you by email at least 30 days before any material change takes effect. The "last revised" date at the top of this page is authoritative.
