// 05 — FAQ
Questions we get most.
How long does it take to deploy Hermes?

About 5 minutes to connect your existing Telegram Bot. 2-4 weeks if you want a fully custom persona tuned on your existing conversation transcripts.

Is my customer data sent to OpenAI / Anthropic?

By default, no. We host open-weight models (Qwen, Llama, Mistral) on HK infrastructure. If you want a hosted model, we offer a fully-logged, zero-retention agreement with the upstream provider.

What happens if the agent doesn't know an answer?

It hands off to a human, with full context. No hallucinations pushed to your customer. You decide the handoff triggers.

Does Hermes support Mandarin / English too?

Yes — trilingual by default, including code-switching between Cantonese and English in a single conversation.
